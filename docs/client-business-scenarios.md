# Everyday cafe tasks — simple client examples

Use your agreed real figures. The numbers below explain the process; they are not records to import. Employee records and system logins are separate. The counter operator uses their counter login; the owners retain administration access.

## 1. Add an ingredient
Open All tools & administration → Stock levels → Add ingredient. Enter a name, such as Flour. Choose Weight, Liquid or Pieces. Set a low-stock alert, such as 2 kg. Save ingredient.

This creates the ingredient and its alert. It does not add imaginary stock. Use Purchases for a supplier delivery. The original stock measure stays fixed when editing, so existing history is preserved.

## 2. Receive a supplier delivery
First add the supplier's name and phone in Supplier balances. Email/address are optional.

In Receive purchases, select the supplier and enter their invoice/receipt number. Add each ingredient, the amount received and its unit. Example: 2 litres of milk costing Rs. 600 means Quantity 2, Unit L, Line total 600.

If you paid Rs. 400, enter Paid now 400 and select the actual payment account. The live summary shows Total 600, Paid 400, Still owed 200. Enter zero if nothing was paid. The invoice date defaults to today; More details lets you check/change it and add notes.

Review the invoice, then Confirm & save. Stock and the supplier balance update together. Use supplier payments for a later payment; do not receive the same invoice again.

## 3. Set a menu recipe
Open Recipes & costing and choose the menu item. For a single cup or plate, keep Portions made by this batch at 1. Add each ingredient and its quantity.

For a batch, enter the total ingredients and the number of portions. Example: 1 kg flour for 10 portions means 100 g for each sale. The review shows the batch and the quantity per sale. Confirm & save after checking it. Non-food services can keep ingredient tracking off.

New orders reserve the recipe quantities; preparation consumes them. Existing orders retain their original snapshots. Cost/price suggestions are available separately when needed.

## 4. Record waste or count stock
In Stock levels, search for the ingredient and use Record waste / count. Waste removes the amount entered. Physical count means the actual total remaining, not the difference. Enter the quantity in kg/L/pieces, give a reason and review before saving.

Use Count stock for a batch of ingredients. Blank means skip; zero means nothing remains. Saving a count creates a draft, without changing stock. Review and post that draft only after checking all quantities. Existing open orders can reserve stock, so review those before reducing it.

## 5. Record gas, electricity, rent or a repair
Open Record expenses. Keep Business expense selected, enter the actual amount, choose the money account, category and date, and write a short reason. Review, then Confirm & save. Supplier payments and salary payments have their own screens. Owner transfers and withdrawals are separate transaction choices.

## 6. Add an employee and mark attendance
Open Employees & salaries. Enter the name, job, salary basis, agreed pay and joining date. Example: Monthly salary with 35000 means a full monthly salary of Rs. 35,000. Daily wage with 1500 means Rs. 1,500 per working day.

Save employee. A code is generated when left blank. More details contains optional codes, overtime rates, notes, leaving dates and login setup. Do not create a login for someone who will not use the system.

In Attendance, find the employee and select Present, Absent or the appropriate leave. Use the detailed form for times, half days, corrections and overtime. Payroll remains Draft → Review/post → Record actual salary payment.

## 7. Manage riders
Open Riders & deliveries. Add the rider's name and phone; a code is generated if left blank. Assign the rider to a delivery, follow the delivery status and record actual rider cash received. Commission and cash held are separate balances. Review cash/commission transactions before confirming them. Saving a rider does not itself pay commission.

## 8. Download details
Each business screen has History dates & Excel downloads. Open it, choose the dates and select the report. Download Excel creates that report's file; Download All creates a ZIP containing separate reports. Synchronize saved offline bills first. Current stock and balances stay current even when dated history is filtered.

## 9. Correct an input
The review screen has Back to edit. No record is posted until Confirm & save. After successful saving, forms reset to help prevent accidental repeat entries. If a response is uncertain, preserve the original record/request and check the error; do not submit a second copy with changed details. Posted transactions use supported returns, refunds or corrections rather than deleting financial history.
