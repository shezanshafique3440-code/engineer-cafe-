# Cafe counter — quick start

## Start the day
Sign in to the counter account. Open **Cash closing**, count the cash already in the drawer and open your own shift. Return to **New sale**. Keep the internet connected until **Offline counter ready** appears.

## Take an order
Choose items and quantities. Check the final bill, enter the cash actually received and check the change. Select **Record payment & send ticket** once. Print the receipt after confirmation. **Orders** shows the full order and payment status. Table orders and riders are in **All tools & administration**.

## Follow the kitchen
Open **Kitchen**. Mark a ticket **Preparing**, then **Ready**. Record any unpaid collection before handing the order over. Complete the handover in the order screen. A payment and a completed sale are different accounting events.

## If the connection stops
The prepared counter opens the saved cash screen. Your current draft stays on this device. Save each cash bill and print its provisional receipt. On reconnect, keep the page open while bills synchronize. **Saved bills** shows pending bills and bills needing review. A provisional receipt is not server confirmation. An uncertain online bill keeps its original ID: use **Check & recover bill**, rather than starting another copy.

## Finish the day
Confirm the last counter bill and synchronize saved bills. Review table balances and rider cash. Open **Cash closing**, check other devices, count the actual drawer cash and explain any difference before closing. The local check covers this browser/login only. Keep an offline bill's original cashier and shift available until it is reconciled.

## Owner tasks
Open **All tools & administration** for reports/Excel files, purchases, ingredients/recipes, expenses, employee records, attendance, salaries, website settings, access, demo-data preview and system health. Employee records do not require a login for every employee. Use separate owner accounts. Review stock, balances and demo-data cleanup before starting real trading.

## Need help?
An error or missing confirmation does not mean the bill failed. Note its bill ID and the message; recover or review the same bill. Keep device storage intact while any bills are pending. Online-only tools require a connection. On a new device or domain, prepare the counter online before relying on offline billing.
